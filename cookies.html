Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This policy explains how RegistryReady uses cookies and similar browser technologies. It should be read with the Privacy Policy.

Essential storage

The site may use limited browser storage to remember interface preferences or consent choices. These functions support the requested experience and are not used to create an operator-owned profile of the text you enter.

Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, and similar technologies to deliver, limit, personalize, and measure ads, prevent fraud, and produce reporting. Depending on your location and consent choices, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

Your choices

Where required, a consent message provides choices before non-essential advertising storage is used. You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser, review Google My Ad Center, and use Google’s advertising opt-out controls. Blocking storage may affect ads or saved preferences but the core tool should remain usable.

Third-party information

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services in Google’s partner-sites policy. Google’s own policies control its technologies.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as technology or legal requirements evolve. Questions can be submitted through the contact page.
